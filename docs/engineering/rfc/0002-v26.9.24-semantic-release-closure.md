## 5. SA2A closure

SA2A closes the machine-to-machine boundary by preserving:

```text
candidate != admitted truth
message != authority
planner output != authority
SELECT != CONSTRUCT != DO
```

A process finding may become an admitted powerless candidate only after exact producer, evidence, semantic-subject, horizon, vocabulary, and capability admission. Consequential execution remains behind CommandBus/BRCE and independently observed postconditions.

## 6. STOGAF closure

## 7. Formal and receipt adapters

BRCE→TLA+ and BRCE→Affidavit are complete protocol adapter specifications even when implementation is not yet ALIVE.

That distinction is intentional:

```text
FINAL_SPEC + PLANNED_IMPLEMENTATION = closed RFC, open implementation obligation
```

No draft-spec zombie remains necessary merely because executable follow-on work exists.

## 8. Cross-product closure

`chatman-ecosystem` owns evaluation of relations between independently bounded evidence dimensions. It does not absorb ownership of those dimensions.

A cross-product PASS may establish compositional correspondence for the declared case. It cannot upgrade producer evidence, grant authority, or prove unmodeled behavior.
