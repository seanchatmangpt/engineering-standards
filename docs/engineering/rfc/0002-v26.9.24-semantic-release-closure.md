~~~text
    FINAL_SPEC

~~~

Implementation closure:

~~~text
STOP_IMPL =
  for every required executable subject:
    ALIVE
    | PARTIAL_ALIVE

    | NOT_CLAIMED
    | REFUSED(type)
    | BLOCKED(type)
~~~

Distribution closure is independent:

~~~text
STOP_DIST =
  every declared publish/tag subject is either
    PUBLISHED(exact artifact/ref)

~~~

A release must never infer distribution merely because a merge exists.

## 11. Primary falsifiers

This closure is falsified if:
1. any required subject remains UNKNOWN;
2. a transient PR head remains pinned after a durable merge subject exists;
3. FINAL_SPEC is used as implementation evidence;
4. a required court failure is concealed rather than repaired or typed as baseline/blocker;
5. a superseded subject lacks an exact successor;
6. authority claimed exceeds its admitted ceiling;
7. a package, release, deployment, or external outcome is claimed without an observable receipt;
8. the executable closure projection disagrees with this normative ledger.
